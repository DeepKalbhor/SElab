Maratha Kings

Legendary Era of Swarajya

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj

The visionary founder of the Maratha Empire. He established Hindavi Swarajya through brilliant guerrilla warfare, strategic fort building, and progressive administration based on justice and equality.

Chhatrapati Sambhaji Maharaj

The fearless second Chhatrapati. Renowned for his supreme valor, he successfully defended the empire against massive invading forces, remaining undefeated in over 120 battles throughout his reign.
