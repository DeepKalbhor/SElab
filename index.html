Shivaji & Sambhaji
The Eternal Glow of Swarajya
The Sun of Swarajya

Shivaji Shahaji Bhonsle

The founding light of the Maratha Empire, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj carved out an independent enclave from declining sultanates, establishing a legacy of strategic brilliance and progressive governance.

"Freedom is a boon, which everyone has the right to enjoy."

His innovative military tactics, fort infrastructure architectures, and radical naval strategies mirrored the fierce, unyielding energy of a midday sun.

The Radiant Successor

Sambhaji Shivaji Bhonsle

The second Chhatrapati, Chhatrapati Sambhaji Maharaj, inherited a colossal crown and defended it with supreme valor, facing multi-front conflicts against empires with unmatched defiance.

"Even if the sky falls, the resolve of a warrior remains unshaken."

A brilliant scholar and undefeated military commander, his fierce sacrifice cemented a structural revolution that kept the fire of independence burning bright.
