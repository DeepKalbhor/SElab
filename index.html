The Founder

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj

Visionary strategist and the legendary founder of the Maratha Empire. He revolutionized warfare by perfecting Ganimi Kava (guerrilla tactics) and built a powerful naval force, establishing an independent, self-ruling sovereign state built on justice and equality.

The Successor

Chhatrapati Sambhaji Maharaj

The second Chhatrapati, renowned for his fierce courage and profound intellect. Undefeated in over 120 battles, he successfully protected the realm against overwhelming external empires, leaving behind a legacy of supreme sacrifice and unwavering valor.
